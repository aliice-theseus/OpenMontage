## 基础运镜

| 运镜 | 英文 | 功能 |
|---|---|---|
| 推 | `push in`, `dolly in` | 聚焦、逼近内心、紧张升级 |
| 拉 | `pull back`, `dolly out` | 揭示环境、孤独、抽离 |
| 摇 | `pan left/right` | 扫视空间、跟随视线 |
| 横移 | `tracking shot`, `truck left/right` | 平行伴随、空间揭示 |
| 跟 | `follow shot from behind` | 代入主角、制造悬念 |
| 升 | `crane up`, `pedestal up` | 打开格局、增强史诗感 |
| 降 | `crane down`, `pedestal down` | 从宏观落到具体、建立压迫 |
| 甩 | `whip pan` | 高能转场、时空切换 |

## 高级运镜

| 技法 | 英文 | 使用约束 |
|---|---|---|
| 环绕 | `orbit shot` | 产品或人物展示；需稳定主体和足够时长 |
| 希区柯克变焦 | `dolly zoom`, `vertigo effect` | 世界观崩塌或眩晕瞬间，少量使用 |
| FPV 穿越 | `FPV drone shot flying through` | 高能穿越；路径必须明确且空间足够 |
| 手持 | `handheld camera, slight shake` | 纪实紧迫；控制幅度避免不可读 |
| 斯坦尼康 | `steadicam, smooth gliding movement` | 沉浸式跟随和长镜头 |
| 升格 | `slow motion, 120fps look` | 强调关键高潮或材质细节 |
| 速度渐变 | `speed ramp, fast to slow` | 强调动作接触点或转折 |
| 低空贴地 | `low-angle glide just above ground` | 速度感、赛道、水面 |
| 穿前景 | `camera moves through foreground objects` | 深度、窥视和自然转场 |
| 一镜到底 | `continuous single take, seamless` | 路径和节拍必须可执行，避免事件过载 |

## 景别系统

| 景别 | 缩写 | 叙事功能 |
|---|---|---|
| 大远景 | EWS | 世界观、规模、渺小感 |
| 远景 | WS | 环境与人物关系 |
| 中景 | MS | 对话、动作和叙事主力 |
| 中近景 | MCU | 情绪聚焦 |
| 特写 | CU | 表情和关键道具 |
| 大特写 | ECU | 眼神和细节爆点 |

## 情绪映射

| 情绪 | 主运镜 | 辅助条件 |
|---|---|---|
| 紧张压迫 | `slow push in` | 克制手持、低角度、阴影加深 |
| 孤独失落 | `slow pull back` | 大留白、冷调、小主体 |
| 兴奋高能 | `FPV`, `whip pan`, `speed ramp` | 清晰路径和节拍落点 |
| 庄重史诗 | `slow crane up` | 远景、体积光、宽画幅 |
| 温馨治愈 | `steadicam slow glide` | 柔光、暖调、浅景深 |
| 悬念不安 | `follow from behind` | 斜角、声音留白、受限视野 |

## 提示模板

### 人物出场

`Low-angle medium shot, slow steady push in toward a woman walking through subway steam. Keep her centered and sharp while the backlit environment drifts softly behind her.`

### 情绪特写

`Extreme close-up on the eyes, static camera with only subtle handheld breathing. The subject remains anchored; shallow focus and soft side window light carry the emotion.`

### 揭示反转

`Begin on a close-up of a smiling family photograph, then slowly pull straight back to reveal the empty dusty room around it. One continuous movement, cold restrained tone.`

### FPV 穿越

`FPV camera flies steadily through one open window, levels out inside the library, then follows the central aisle between shelves. Clear unobstructed path, continuous take, no abrupt yaw, no warping.`

### 甩镜转场

`Fast whip pan right from the office desk, full-frame motion blur, landing on a beach horizon moving in the same direction. One transition beat with a clearly matched endpoint.`
