## 色调方案

| 方案 | 用途 | 提示锚点 |
|---|---|---|
| 青橙 | 商业大片、人物、城市 | `teal and orange color grading, complementary contrast` |
| 胶片暖调 | 生活、亲情、美食 | `warm film tone, golden glow, soft highlights` |
| 莫兰迪灰 | 家居、穿搭、文艺 | `muted morandi palette, desaturated dusty pastel, gray undertone` |
| 冷蓝灰 | 悬疑、科技、疏离 | `cold blue-gray tone, desaturated, steel-blue shadows` |
| 赛博霓虹 | 夜景、科幻、电音 | `magenta and cyan practical glow, wet reflections` |
| 黑金 | 品牌、珠宝、酒类 | `black and gold palette, deep shadows, warm tungsten highlights` |
| 日系清透 | 清晨、校园、治愈 | `airy Japanese aesthetic, soft pastel, slightly overexposed, low contrast` |
| 复古褪色 | 回忆、旧街、年代感 | `vintage faded film, subtle halation, restrained grain` |

色调公式：

`[主色倾向] + [强调色关系] + [饱和度与对比度] + [单一风格参照]`

## 光位与光质

| 光位/光质 | 表达 | 叙事效果 |
|---|---|---|
| 侧光 | `side lighting`, `Rembrandt lighting` | 立体、人物故事感 |
| 逆光 | `backlight`, `rim light`, `silhouette` | 氛围、神秘、轮廓分离 |
| 侧逆光 | `kicker light`, `three-quarter backlight` | 高级人像与轮廓 |
| 顶光 | `overhead light` | 压迫、审讯、戏剧性 |
| 窗光 | `soft window light from the side` | 文艺、生活流、静物 |
| 硬光 | `hard key light, sharp shadows` | 力量、冲突、锐利 |
| 柔光 | `soft diffused light` | 温和、护肤品级人像 |
| 体积光 | `volumetric light, light shafts through dust` | 空气感、神圣或史诗 |
| 斑驳光 | `dappled light through leaves` | 自然、日系、文艺 |

时间光线：`golden hour` 温暖万能；`blue hour` 静谧忧郁；`harsh midday sun` 纪实燥热；`overcast soft ambient light` 情绪柔和；`practical tungsten lights` 夜间反差。

## 构图和留白

| 构图 | 表达 | 作用 |
|---|---|---|
| 三分法 | `rule of thirds` | 通用主体组织 |
| 中心对称 | `centered symmetrical composition` | 仪式、秩序、压迫 |
| 留白 | `generous negative space` | 呼吸、孤独、标题空间 |
| 引导线 | `leading lines` | 纵深和视线指向 |
| 框中框 | `frame within a frame` | 窥视、约束、层次 |
| 对角线 | `diagonal composition` | 运动和张力 |
| 前景遮挡 | `foreground occlusion` | 深度、观察感、自然转场 |

留白可承担三种任务：

- 呼吸：让 40%–60% 画面保持低信息密度。
- 指向：在视线或运动方向前方保留 `lead room / looking space`。
- 延伸：结尾使用小主体和大空间，把情绪留给观众。

## 材质与镜头感

- `subtle 35mm film grain`：减少纯数码感，保持克制。
- `shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh`：主体分离。
- `anamorphic lens, oval bokeh, restrained horizontal flare`：宽银幕镜头感。
- `subtle handheld breathing`：避免完全机械的静止。
- `visible pores, natural skin texture`：抑制塑料皮肤。
- `fabric weave`, `brushed metal`, `natural wood grain`：特写表面信息。
- `atmospheric haze, dust particles in light`：空气层次。

## 组合模板

### 情绪人物特写

`Close-up by a window, soft side window light with restrained Rembrandt contrast, muted low-saturation palette, negative space toward the subject's gaze, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, subtle 35mm grain.`

### 黑金产品

`Extreme close-up of the product on black stone, black-and-gold palette, one hard key light creating controlled specular highlights, deep clean shadows, brushed-metal texture, a single restrained light shaft, luxury commercial treatment.`

### 史诗远景

`Extreme wide landscape at dawn, cold blue-gray base with one warm sunrise accent, volumetric rays through haze, a lone small figure, vast intentional negative space, aerial perspective, restrained cinematic contrast.`

### 负面约束

按工具能力选择：`oversaturated colors, plastic skin, muddy shadows, random light sources, excessive lens flare, blurry texture, distorted anatomy`。
